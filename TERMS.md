## Privacy features have limits

- Ad and tracker blocking and anti-fingerprinting reduce tracking. They do not make you anonymous.
- The free-relay VPN uses public relays run by unknown third parties. They may log, slow down or interfere with your traffic. Do not use them for logins, payments or anything sensitive.
- Tor gives stronger anonymity, but what you do on a site (for example signing in) can still identify you.
- Private tabs stop Gecko from keeping data on your phone. They do not hide your activity from websites, your network or your internet provider.
